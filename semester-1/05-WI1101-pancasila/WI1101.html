    KategoriKuliah
    Bahasa IndonesiaEnglish
    Nama Mata KuliahFisika Dasar IGeneral Physics I
    Bahan Kajian
    1. Pengukuran dan satuan
    2. Kinematika (gerak dalam 1, 2 dan 3 dimensi)
    3. Dinamika (hukum Newton – gaya dan gerak benda)
    4. Usaha dan energi, hukum kekekalan energi
    5. Impuls dan momentum, hukum kekekalan momentum
    6. Gerak rotasi (kinematika dan dinamika gerak rotasi)
    7. Elastisitas dan osilasi
    1. Measurements and units
    2. Kinematics (motion in 1, 2 and 3 dimensions)
    3. Dynamics (Newton's laws – force and motion of objects)
    4. Work and energy, law of conservation of energy
    5. Impulse and momentum, law of conservation of momentum
    6. Rotational motion (kinematics and dynamics of rotational motion)
    7. Elasticity and oscillations
    Capaian Pembelajaran Mata Kuliah (CPMK)
    1. Mampu memformulasikan besaran-besaran fisika yang berkaitan dengan gerak benda dalam model fisis menggunakan matematika
    2. Mampu menerapkan konsep/prinsip/hukum terkait kinematika, dinamika dan energi (mekanika Newton) untuk memecahkan persoalan suatu sistem fisis
    3. Mampu berpikir kritis, logis, sistematis dan terstruktur dalam menyelesaikan masalah-masalah sistem fisis
    1. Able to formulate physical quantities related to the motion of objects in physical models using mathematics
    2. Able to apply concepts/principles/laws related to kinematics, dynamics and energy (Newtonian mechanics) to solve problems of a physical system
    3. Able to think critically, logically, systematically and structured in solving physical system problems
