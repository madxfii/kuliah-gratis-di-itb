## Bagian 1: Aplikasi CT dalam Domain Spesifik

### 1.1 Data Science & Analytics

**Skenario Nyata**: Perusahaan e-commerce ingin tahu tren penjualan & behavior pelanggan untuk optimize marketing.

**Bagaimana CT Digunakan**:

| CT Pilar | Aplikasi | Contoh |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Dekomposisi** | Break data problem: collect → clean → explore → model → interpret | Separate data: by product, region, time period |
| **Pattern Recognition** | Identify regularities dalam customer behavior | "Produk A dijual 3x lebih banyak pada akhir bulan" |
| **Abstraction** | Focus pada metrics penting: revenue, growth rate | Ignore: exact timestamps, individual buyer names |
| **Algorithm Design** | Process pipeline: ETL → Statistical Analysis → Prediction | Build recommendation algorithm |

**Hasil yang Dicapai**:
- Personalized product recommendations
- Inventory optimization
- Marketing strategy based on data
- Revenue increase 20-40%

**Skills yang Diperlukan**: Python, SQL, statistics, business sense

**Career Opportunity**: Data Analyst, Data Scientist (salary: $70K-150K+)

---

### 1.2 Cybersecurity

**Skenario Nyata**: Perusahaan bank harus protect customer data dari cyber attacks.

**Bagaimana CT Digunakan**:

| CT Pilar | Aplikasi | Contoh |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Dekomposisi** | Break security into layers: network → application → data | Design multi-layered defense |
| **Pattern Recognition** | Identify attack signatures dari historical data | "Ini pattern dari phishing attack yang pernah terjadi" |
| **Abstraction** | Focus pada vulnerability, threat level | Ignore: attacker's personal motivation |
| **Algorithm Design** | Detection system: monitor → alert → respond | Anomaly detection algorithm |

**Hasil yang Dicapai**:
- Prevent unauthorized access
- Detect breaches early
- Respond quickly to threats
- Compliance dengan regulations

**Skills yang Diperlukan**: Networking, cryptography, threat analysis, penetration testing

**Career Opportunity**: Cybersecurity Analyst, Security Engineer (salary: $80K-160K+)

---

### 1.3 Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

**Skenario Nyata**: Rumah sakit ingin diagnosa kanker lebih akurat menggunakan medical imaging AI.

**Bagaimana CT Digunakan**:

| CT Pilar | Aplikasi | Contoh |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Dekomposisi** | Break ML pipeline: data collection → feature engineering → model training → evaluation | Separate tasks untuk team members |
| **Pattern Recognition** | Identify patterns dalam medical images untuk detect cancer | Neural network learns subtle patterns humans miss |
| **Abstraction** | Represent image sebagai features (edges, textures, shapes) | Reduce high-dimensional data |
| **Algorithm Design** | Design neural network architecture | Layers, parameters, optimization strategy |

**Hasil yang Dicapai**:
- Earlier cancer detection
- Higher accuracy diagnosis
- Better patient outcomes
- Reduced doctor workload

**Skills yang Diperlukan**: Python, Machine Learning, Statistics, Domain knowledge (medical)

**Career Opportunity**: ML Engineer, AI Researcher (salary: $100K-180K+)

---

### 1.4 Healthcare & Bioinformatics

**Skenario Nyata**: Ilmuwan ingin identify genetic mutations yang cause rare disease untuk develop treatment.

**Bagaimana CT Digunakan**:

| CT Pilar | Aplikasi | Contoh |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Dekomposisi** | Break genomic analysis: sequence alignment → mutation detection → pathway analysis | Handle huge datasets systematically |
| **Pattern Recognition** | Identify mutation patterns yang correlate dengan disease | "This mutation appears in 95% of patients" |
| **Abstraction** | Focus pada significant mutations, ignore noise | DNA sequence ≈ 3 billion characters, focus on ~1000 variants |
| **Algorithm Design** | GWAS (Genome-Wide Association Study) algorithm | Correlate genetic data with disease phenotype |

**Hasil yang Dicapai**:
- Discover disease-causing mutations
- Personalized medicine
- New treatment development
- Save lives

**Skills yang Diperlukan**: Bioinformatics, genomics, programming, statistics

**Career Opportunity**: Bioinformatician, Computational Biologist (salary: $70K-140K+)

---

### 1.5 Sustainable Computing & Climate Science

**Skenario Nyata**: Ilmuwan ingin predict climate change & evaluate impact dari policy interventions.

**Bagaimana CT Digunakan**:

| CT Pilar | Aplikasi | Contoh |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Dekomposisi** | Break Earth system: atmosphere → ocean → land interactions | Model each component separately |
| **Pattern Recognition** | Identify climate patterns dari historical data | "Temperature trend is increasing 0.2°C per decade" |
| **Abstraction** | Model complex physics dengan equations & parameters | Reduce global climate to computable model |
| **Algorithm Design** | Climate simulation: run model for centuries, vary inputs | Predict future scenarios |

**Hasil yang Dicapai**:
- Accurate climate predictions
- Inform policy decisions
- Evaluate effectiveness dari interventions
- Save planet for future generations

**Skills yang Diperlukan**: Physics, environmental science, numerical modeling, HPC (high-performance computing)

**Career Opportunity**: Climate Scientist, Environmental Engineer (salary: $60K-130K+)

### 1.6 Game Design & Creative Computing

**Skenario Nyata**: Studio game ingin develop multiplayer game yang fun, fair, dan scalable untuk 1 juta players.

## Bagian 2: Tren Terkini (2026) & Implikasi

### 2.1 AI & Machine Learning Evolution

**Trend**: Agentic AI, multimodal models, LLMs generative

**Implikasi untuk CT**:
- CT abstraction layers shift — less focus on low-level details, more on data/patterns
- Need to understand how AI works (even if using pre-trained models)
- New problems: bias detection, fairness, interpretability

**Implikasi untuk Career**:
- New jobs: Prompt engineering, AI safety, responsible AI
- Existing jobs transform: programmers now collaborate with AI
- Skill requirements evolve rapidly

**Question for Reflection**: "Jika AI bisa code, apa role dari programmer di masa depan?"

---

### 2.2 Edge Computing & IoT

**Trend**: Processing data at source (edge) not in cloud

**Why**: Latency, privacy, bandwidth efficiency

**Implikasi untuk CT**:
- Decomposition of monolithic systems into distributed edge+cloud
- New constraints: optimize for limited resources (battery, memory, compute)
- Tradeoff: cloud power vs. edge efficiency

**Real-World Example**: Autonomous vehicle
- Camera/LIDAR sensors (edge)
- Local processing (edge): detect obstacles quickly
- Cloud communication (selective): send only important data

**Career Opportunity**: Edge Computing Engineer, IoT Specialist

---

### 2.3 Quantum Computing

**Trend**: Quantum computers available from cloud providers (IBM, Google, AWS)

**Why**: Exponentially faster untuk certain problems (encryption, optimization)

**Implikasi untuk CT**:
- Algorithmic thinking must adapt: superposition, entanglement principles
- But decomposition & abstraction still critical
- Hybrid approach: classical + quantum algorithms

**Challenge**: Masalah apa yang quantum bisa solve better? (Still being discovered)

**Career Opportunity**: Quantum Algorithm Researcher, Quantum Software Engineer (emerging field)

---

### 2.4 Natural Language Programming & AI-Assisted Development

**Trend**: "Write code using English" — ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot

**Impact**: 
- Non-technical people can create software
- Programmers more productive (AI assists)
- But prompting is a skill! (specify problem clearly = CT skill!)

**Implikasi untuk CT**:
- Abstraction layer shifts higher (English → code)
- Logical thinking (CT) STILL needed untuk specify problems correctly
- Emphasis on problem formulation, not syntax

**Example**: 
```
Prompt: "Create function to find median of array"
AI generates: working code instantly

But: Programmer must understand what "median" means,
when to use it, how to validate output (CT skills!)
```

---

### 2.5 Ethical Computing & Responsible AI

**Trend**: Regulations, ethical guidelines, responsible AI frameworks emerging

**Why**: AI can perpetuate bias, harm vulnerable populations, used for surveillance

**Examples**:
- Hiring algorithm discriminates against women (bias in training data)
- Facial recognition misidentifies minorities (accuracy gap)
- Predictive policing over-polices certain neighborhoods (societal bias)

**Implikasi untuk CT**:
- Algorithm designers must consider: fairness, transparency, accountability
- Not just "does it work?" but "does it work fairly for everyone?"
- Need interdisciplinary approach: CS + ethics + social science

**Career Opportunity**: AI Ethics Officer, Responsible AI Engineer (growing field)

## Bagian 3: Dampak Sosial & Critical Thinking

### 3.1 Positive Impacts dari Computing

✓ **Efficiency**: Automate tasks, free humans untuk creative work
✓ **Innovation**: Solve previously impossible problems (drug discovery, climate modeling)
✓ **Accessibility**: Technology makes world more accessible untuk people dengan disabilities
✓ **Connection**: Global communication, knowledge sharing
✓ **Healthcare**: Better diagnosis, treatment, personalized medicine
✓ **Education**: Online learning, reach remote areas

### 3.2 Negative Impacts & Challenges

⚠ **Job Displacement**: Automation eliminates jobs, workers struggle to retrain
⚠ **Digital Divide**: Not everyone has access to technology, widening inequality
⚠ **Surveillance**: Data collection, privacy concerns, government monitoring
⚠ **Bias & Discrimination**: AI reflects training data biases
⚠ **Environmental Cost**: Data centers consume massive energy
⚠ **Mental Health**: Social media addiction, misinformation spread

### 3.3 Studi Kasus: Dampak Real-World

#### Kasus 1: Predictive Policing

**Teknologi**: Algoritma prediksi crime berdasarkan historical crime data

**Positive**: 
- Help police allocate resources efficiently
- Potentially prevent crimes

**Negative**:
- Historical data reflects biased policing practices
- Algorithm perpetuates discrimination
- Over-policing tertentu neighborhoods

**Ethical Question**: "Siapa yang responsible jika algorithm membuat keputusan tidak fair?"

#### Kasus 2: Facial Recognition

**Teknologi**: Identify people dari photos/video

**Positive**:
- Security (find missing persons, catch criminals)
- Convenience (unlock phone)

**Negative**:
- Surveillance state potential
- Accuracy varies by race (trained mostly on white faces)
- Used untuk oppression (China's Uyghur surveillance)

**Ethical Question**: "Accuracy untuk semua racial groups penting? Bagaimana memastikan?"

#### Kasus 3: Social Media Algorithm

**Teknologi**: Recommend content yang likely user engage

**Positive**:
- Personalized experience
- Discover new content

**Negative**:
- Echo chambers: people only see views they agree with
- Misinformation spreads faster than truth
- Addiction designed into system
- Mental health impact (especially teens)

**Ethical Question**: "Is maximizing engagement always good? What about broader social impact?"

## Bagian 4: Role & Responsibility dari Computing Professionals

### 4.1 Ethical Frameworks untuk Designer/Developer

**Framework 1: Consequentialism**
- "Bagaimana outcome dari technology ini untuk society?"
- Evaluate both intended & unintended consequences

**Framework 2: Deontology**
- "Apa duties/obligations kita sebagai professionals?"
- Some actions inherently wrong (vs. outcome-based evaluation)

**Framework 3: Virtue Ethics**
- "What kind of person/professional do I want to be?"
- Focus on character & values, not just rules

### 4.2 Tanggung Jawab Praktis

**Personal Level**:
- [ ] Learn about ethical implications dari teknologi Anda develop
- [ ] Speak up if you see unethical practices
- [ ] Continue learning, don't assume current knowledge is sufficient
- [ ] Consider diverse perspectives, especially dari affected communities

**Organizational Level**:
- [ ] Advocate untuk ethical practices dalam company
- [ ] Participate dalam ethics review processes
- [ ] Support underrepresented groups dalam tech industry
- [ ] Transparency dengan users tentang how technology works

**Societal Level**:
- [ ] Contribute ke open-source projects (democratize technology)
- [ ] Mentor next generation, especially dari underrepresented backgrounds
- [ ] Engage dengan policy makers (technology ≠ neutral, regulations needed)
- [ ] Use technology untuk social good (not just profit)

## Bagian 5: Equity & Inclusion dalam Tech

### Issue: Tech Industry Lacks Diversity

**Statistics** (2024):
- Only ~25% dalam tech workforce adalah women
- Only ~10% adalah Black/Hispanic
- Disabled people severely underrepresented
- LGBTQ+ face discrimination

### Why Diversity Matters

1. **Better products**: Diverse teams catch blindspots, design untuk broader audience
2. **Better ethics**: More perspectives consider ethical implications
3. **Justice**: Everyone should have opportunity dalam high-paying field
4. **Innovation**: Diverse backgrounds bring diverse ideas

### What You Can Do

- [ ] Support peers dari underrepresented backgrounds
- [ ] Advocate untuk inclusive hiring & mentorship
- [ ] Learn about unconscious bias, actively counter it
- [ ] Choose companies dengan commitment to diversity
- [ ] If you're in majority: use privilege to amplify underheard voices
